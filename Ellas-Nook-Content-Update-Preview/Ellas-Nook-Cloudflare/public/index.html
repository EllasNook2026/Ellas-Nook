The Story Behind Ella’s Nook

A Name That Feels Like Comfort

The name Ella has a little story of its own.

In Keeper of the Lost Cities, Ella is a soft toy that brings comfort to Sophie when things feel overwhelming. We loved the thought of having something familiar and comforting to turn to — a little reminder that sometimes, it’s the simplest things that help us feel better.

The name also holds a personal connection to our family and memories of Sathya’s father, Ellappan, whose spirit of hoping for the best continues to inspire us.

That feeling is at the heart of Ella’s Nook, too.

A place where you can pause, try something new, enjoy the company around you and leave with a little something — perhaps a handmade creation, a new idea or simply a happier moment.

Welcome to Ella’s Nook.

What Is Ella’s Nook?

A Space to Make Your Own

Ella’s Nook is not just about learning a craft. It’s about enjoying the experience of making something, exploring your interests and giving yourself time to do what feels right.

Some days, you might want to learn a new technique. On other days, you might prefer to sit quietly with a book, work on a project you brought along or try something simply because it looks interesting. You can do that here.

For curious beginners

Never tried painting, clay or crochet? Start with the basics, learn at your own pace and enjoy the process without worrying about getting everything right.

For families

Bring the children along to explore something new while you enjoy creative time of your own. Make something together or choose different activities.

For friends and groups

Swap the usual outing for hands-on creativity. Make something together, try something unfamiliar and enjoy time away from everyday routines.

For teams and organisations

Ella’s Nook can host creative group experiences that offer a refreshing change from the usual meeting-room setting.

For anyone who needs a little pause

You don’t always need a special occasion or a planned activity. Sometimes, a change of surroundings and a little time for yourself are reason enough to visit.

From children aged five and above to adults of all ages, everyone is welcome at Ella’s Nook.

Open Creative Time

No Workshop? No Problem.

Not every visit needs a timetable.

Open Creative Time is for those moments when you simply want to do something with your hands, explore a craft or spend time in a creative space without signing up for a structured workshop.

Come in, explore the available activities and choose what you feel like doing. You could try a craft, continue something you’ve been working on or spend a little time with a book or puzzle. You can come alone or bring someone along.

You don’t have to know exactly what you want to make before you arrive. Sometimes, the best ideas happen once you get started.

A Space That Keeps Changing

There’s Always Room for a New Idea

Ella’s Nook is growing one little experiment at a time. New crafts find their way onto our tables. New faces walk through the door. Visitors bring their own ideas, and sometimes, an ordinary afternoon leads to an entirely new activity.

We want the Nook to remain a space where curiosity is welcome, beginners feel comfortable and there’s always something new to explore.

There’s no need to get everything right. There’s no pressure to turn every interest into a skill or every afternoon into something productive.

Come to try, learn, make, share or simply enjoy being here. We’re happy to see what takes shape next.
